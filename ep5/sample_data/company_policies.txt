ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (Excerpts)
=================================================

REMOTE WORK POLICY
------------------
All full-time employees are eligible for remote work up to 3 days per week. To request remote work, submit a Remote Work Agreement form to your manager at least 5 business days in advance. Remote work days must be pre-approved and logged in the HR portal. Employees working remotely must be available during core hours (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time) and maintain a stable internet connection. Remote work privileges may be revoked if performance standards are not met.

PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
-------------------
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of PTO per calendar year, accruing at 1.67 days per month. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis. Unused PTO may be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days. PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for planned absences. Emergency or sick leave should be reported to your manager by 9:00 AM on the first day of absence. PTO balances can be checked at any time through the HR portal.

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
---------------------
Employees may submit expense reports for business-related costs including travel, meals during business trips, office supplies, and professional development. All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction date. Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Meal expenses are capped at $50 per day for domestic travel and $75 per day for international travel. Hotel accommodations should not exceed $200 per night without prior approval from the department head. Expense reports are processed within 10 business days of submission.

PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
-------------------------
ACME Corporation allocates $2,500 per employee per year for professional development. This can be used for online courses, conferences, certifications, books, and workshops. To use your professional development budget, submit a request through the Learning Portal with the course name, provider, cost, and a brief justification of how it relates to your role. Requests over $500 require manager approval. Approved expenses are reimbursed upon completion and proof of payment. Employees are encouraged to share learnings with their team through a brief presentation or written summary.

CODE OF CONDUCT
---------------
All employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional conduct. This includes treating colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and dignity. Discrimination, harassment, and bullying of any kind are strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Employees must disclose any conflicts of interest to their manager or the ethics committee. Confidential company information must not be shared externally without proper authorization. Violations of the code of conduct should be reported through the anonymous ethics hotline or directly to HR.

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
-------------------
Performance reviews are conducted twice per year — a mid-year check-in in June and a full annual review in December. Reviews include self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback (for roles that interact cross-functionally). Goals are set collaboratively between the employee and their manager at the beginning of each cycle. Performance ratings follow a 5-point scale: Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement, and Unsatisfactory. Employees rated "Needs Improvement" or below will be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) with clear milestones and a 90-day timeline.
